3. Module 1: Petty Cash Management
MODULE 1 — Petty Cash Management

3.1  Purpose and Scope
Module 1 enables Organisations to digitally manage petty cash funds. Its features include:
Creation and configuration of one or more Petty Cash Funds with defined float amounts.
Recording of individual Disbursements against a fund, including payee, purpose, amount, and date.
Uploading of supporting receipts or vouchers for each Disbursement.
Reconciliation of fund balances and generation of reconciliation reports.
Assignment of Custodian and Administrator roles for each fund.
Top-up request and replenishment workflows.
3.2  Custodian Responsibilities
Users designated as Custodians of a Petty Cash Fund agree to:
Maintain accurate and complete records of all Disbursements made from the fund.
Ensure that Supporting Documents (receipts, vouchers) are uploaded promptly for each Disbursement.
Not record fictitious, inflated, or fraudulent Disbursements.
Reconcile the fund balance at such intervals as required by their Organisation or by applicable law.
Report any discrepancy, loss, or suspected misuse of the Petty Cash Fund to their Administrator immediately.
Return or account for any unspent fund balance upon request by the Administrator or Organisation.
3.3  Administrator Responsibilities
Users with Administrator access to Module 1 agree to:
Review and approve or reject top-up and replenishment requests in a timely manner.
Monitor fund activity for irregularities, anomalies, or policy violations.
Configure fund limits, disbursement categories, and approval thresholds appropriately.
Ensure that only authorised individuals are assigned Custodian or Administrator roles.
3.4  Disbursement Limits and Controls
Module 1 may allow Organisations to set per-transaction and per-period disbursement limits. Users acknowledge that:
Minty enforces limits as configured by the Administrator; it is the Organisation's responsibility to set appropriate limits.
Circumventing or splitting transactions to avoid disbursement limits is prohibited and may constitute misconduct under your Organisation's policies.
Minty is not responsible for losses arising from misconfigured limits or insufficient internal controls.
3.5  Record Accuracy and Audit
All Disbursement records entered into Module 1 are intended to accurately reflect real transactions. You agree that:
Records submitted through Module 1 may be used by your Organisation for internal audits, financial reporting, and compliance purposes.
Minty generates Petty Cash reports based solely on data entered by Users; the accuracy of those reports depends on the accuracy of User-entered data.
Minty does not independently audit, verify, or validate petty cash transactions.

4. Module 2: Bill Submission
MODULE 2 — Bill Submission

4.1  Purpose and Scope
Module 2 enables Users to submit bills and expense claims for review and approval within their Organisation. Features include:
Creation and submission of Bill Submissions with itemised expense details, amounts, dates, and categories.
Upload of Supporting Documents (receipts, invoices, purchase orders) to substantiate each submission.
Multi-level approval workflows configurable by Administrators.
Status tracking of submitted bills (pending, approved, rejected, paid).
Generation of expense summary reports for approved submissions.
Integration with petty cash records where an expense is reimbursed from a Petty Cash Fund under Module 1.
4.2  User Obligations — Submitters
Users who submit bills through Module 2 agree to:
Submit only legitimate, business-related expenses that comply with their Organisation's expense policy.
Ensure all details — amounts, dates, payees, and categories — are accurate and truthful.
Upload clear and legible Supporting Documents for each Bill Submission; blurred, altered, or falsified documents are prohibited.
Not submit duplicate claims for the same expense.
Not submit personal or non-business expenses as business claims.
Acknowledge that fraudulent or dishonest expense submissions may result in disciplinary action by the Organisation, and may constitute a criminal offence under Hong Kong law.
4.3  User Obligations — Approvers
Users with approval authority in Module 2 agree to:
Review Bill Submissions promptly and in good faith against the Organisation's expense policy.
Approve only submissions that are legitimate, properly documented, and within authorised limits.
Reject submissions that are incomplete, unsupported, or appear fraudulent, providing a reason for rejection where possible.
Not approve their own Bill Submissions or those submitted by persons they supervise, where a conflict of interest exists.
Maintain the confidentiality of expense information reviewed in their approver capacity.
4.4  Supporting Documents
By uploading Supporting Documents through Module 2, you represent and warrant that:
The documents are authentic and have not been altered, fabricated, or digitally manipulated.
You have the right to upload and share the documents within your Organisation.
The documents accurately reflect the transaction they are intended to support.
Minty reserves the right to flag or reject submissions with suspicious or unreadable Supporting Documents.
4.5  Approval Workflow and Finality
The approval workflow in Module 2 is configured by your Organisation's Administrator. You acknowledge that:
Approval of a Bill Submission within Minty does not by itself authorise payment; actual payment is subject to your Organisation's financial controls.
Approved submissions may be exported, reported on, or integrated with your Organisation's accounting systems.
Once a submission is marked as paid, it may not be edited. Disputes regarding paid submissions must be raised with your Organisation's Administrator.
