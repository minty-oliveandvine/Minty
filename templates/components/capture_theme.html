{# The AI Hub palette — one source of truth. Included by BOTH the bubble partial and the queue page, so the two cannot drift apart. Duplicate :root blocks are harmless in CSS, which is what lets a page that renders the queue AND receives the injected bubble include it twice without consequence. WHY THESE COLOURS ARE NOT THE APP'S `primary` The app's Tailwind `primary` is #294882, a navy. The AI Hub is aqua. That is a deliberate difference, not an oversight: the bubble floats over every page in the application, and a floating control that shares the page's own accent colour reads as part of whatever is underneath it rather than as one consistent thing that follows you around. WHY THE TEXT ON THE AQUA IS DARK, NOT WHITE #54D3DA is a light colour. White text on it measures 1.79:1, where the standard asks 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large or bold — so white fails by a wide margin and reads as a smudge on anything smaller than a heading. Rather than darken the brand colour to make white work, the colour is kept exactly as specified and the text on it is `--cap-on-mint`, which measures 5.56:1. The one place white survives is the bubble's sparkle icon. An icon is a large solid shape rather than text, and white-on-aqua is the look the design asks for. That is a considered exception, not an oversight. Keep every AI Hub colour here. A hex code written inline in a component is how two screens start disagreeing three months from now. #}
