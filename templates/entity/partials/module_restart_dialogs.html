It is now module_card_capture.html, shared with the trial decision modal: both need the same Elements form, and two integrations on one page would be two places for the mandate and the mount to drift. IT LIVES OUTSIDE #restartModal, and that is load-bearing rather than tidiness. ``backdrop-filter`` (the blur on the restart dialog) CREATES A CONTAINING BLOCK for ``position: fixed`` descendants. Nested inside it, it anchored to the restart dialog's own scrolled box instead of the viewport: scroll the card list down, click "Use a different card", and the card form opened half off-screen with its backdrop covering only part of the page. Same trap would apply to a transform, a filter, or ``will-change`` on any ancestor. So it is a sibling of the restart dialog, not a child — which also means removing the blur later cannot silently re-break it. #} {# --- The card dialog -------------------------------------------------------- A SEPARATE, DISMISSIBLE dialog, and the difference from the screen behind it is deliberate. The restart screen cannot be closed because the trial has already lapsed and there is nothing underneath to go back to. Adding a card is an optional detour from it: cancelling just returns the payer to the saved-card list, and the abandoned SetupIntent expires on its own without leaving a customer behind. So this one closes on Cancel, on Escape and on the backdrop, like any other dialog in the app. Outside #restartScreen in the markup so the backdrop covers the whole viewport rather than being clipped by the card it came from. #} {# --- The confirmation dialog ------------------------------------------------ The last thing between the payer and a charge, so it is a dialog rather than a box further down the same card: the amount, the modules and the card all have to be read in one place, and an inline panel below a long card list can open off-screen. DISMISSIBLE, like the add-a-card dialog and unlike the screen behind it. "Back" is not a way out of the restart — it returns to the selection, which is exactly what someone who has just read the amount and changed their mind needs. It restates the SELECTION, not what lapsed. Those can differ, and any edit to either while this is open retracts it (see retractConfirm) rather than leaving a sentence describing a charge that is no longer the one on offer. #}
