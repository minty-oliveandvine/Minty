{# THE SWITCH between the ordinary module page and the lapsed-trial restart screen. Branched here, once, so the two parent templates (settings_module.html and its bills-UI twin) each carry a single include and cannot drift apart. THE ORDINARY PAGE ALWAYS RENDERS. The restart screen sits on top of it as a dialog rather than replacing it — a payer who wants to read what they had, or reach another control, can dismiss it and do so. DISMISSED IS NOT ANSWERED. Nothing is remembered: no storage key, no server flag, no cookie. Closing it hides it for this view of the page and it returns on the next load, every time, until the payer actually restarts or cancels. That is the whole design — it does not block, and it does not go away either. Three states, from ``modules.build_consent_takeover``: * takeover — nothing live is left. * panel — something lapsed but another module is still trialing. * falsy — nothing lapsed, or an entity that still pays for something. Nothing extra. BOTH modes are the same dialog. They differ only in what it says: the panel wording scopes itself to the module that went dark, so a payer whose other trial is running fine does not read it as a threat to everything. Both dismiss, because both leave a page underneath that is worth reaching — in panel mode a live trial with its own controls, in takeover mode the record of what was there. ``can_act`` gates the form everywhere: a co-admin gets the notice naming the payer and never a control ``require_subscription_payer`` would refuse. #}
