Handles the Stripe checkout / trial / cancel / renew / manage-billing redirects (endpoints in blueprints/entity/routes/settings.py). Module access is driven by Stripe — there is no on/off toggle — and the subscription summary is rendered server-side. {# Subscription decision modal — "Choose your modules", the one place the shape of the subscription is chosen, and now the ONLY place: the module cards carry no Start free trial / Subscribe of their own any more. ONE list, for every entity. It used to be two variants of the same markup — a trial one titled "We're glad with your decision!" that listed only what was live, and a paid one titled "Change your modules" that added what the entity did not have. They are merged. Two reasons, and the first is a straight consequence of the cards losing their buttons. The trial variant HID every module the entity did not hold, on the grounds that starting one belonged to the card — so with the cards silent, a trialing entity would have had no way at all to take up a second module. And the split asked the wrong question anyway: it keyed off what the entity is rather than what it is deciding, so the same tick meant the same thing on both and only the wrapper differed. Every row is listed in every state, and what each one does when ticked / unticked is decided by data-keep-state: trial ticked → needs card or consent CANNOT BE UNTICKED (box is disabled) paid ticked → nothing (already on) unticked → prorated cancel, priced+confirmed available ticked → START ITS FREE TRIAL unticked → nothing ...while one is still available. Only a module with no free days left — a spent trial, a lapsed paid subscription — is subscribed and charged. winding ticked → renew unticked → nothing That available row is the one the cards used to duplicate. The card offered a bare "Subscribe" for a module whose free trial was still intact, while this list started the trial for the very same module — one module, two routes, two prices, and which one the customer paid decided by where they happened to click. The card's route is gone. CANCELLING A FREE TRIAL IS NOT OFFERED IN THE UI. It was this modal's untick, and nothing else — the module cards never carried it — so removing the untick removes the only route to it. The server-side cancellation is untouched (``entity_settings_module_cancel`` still accepts a trialing module, and the scheduler and support tooling still reach it); a trial cancelled by any of those still shows up here as a winding row, and re-ticking it is how it is kept. z-[95] — deliberately BELOW the blocking loader (100) and the billing confirm dialog (110), because it stays open while the decision is applied: the loader has to cover it, and the confirmations it raises have to sit on top of it. #} {# The trial's end date — from ANY trialing module, cancelled or not: a cancelled trial still runs to that date, and that is the date its copy has to name. THE EARLIEST ONE. This used to take whichever trialing card came first, and cards are built in ``MODULE_CODES`` order, not date order — so two trials started on different days could put the LATER date on "Your free trial covers … until X", on the plan note, and on the "From X" label of the billing dialog that authorises the conversion. The first thing that happens is the first trial ending; that is the date with a deadline attached to it, and the one the customer can miss. Sorted on ``period_end``, the raw datetime, rather than on the formatted string — "12 Sep 2026" and "3 Oct 2026" do not sort as text. ``selectattr('period_end')`` drops the cards that have no date to sort by. #} {% set trial_end = ((module_cards or []) | selectattr('subscription_status', 'equalto', 'trialing') | selectattr('period_end') | sort(attribute='period_end') | map(attribute='period_end_long') | select | first) %}
