# Minty Privacy Policy

## PLACEHOLDER — NOT A PRIVACY POLICY

This file is a placeholder so that `/legal/privacy` resolves during local
development. It is **not** a privacy policy and has no legal effect.

Section 19 of the Minty Beta Terms of Use refers to "the Minty Privacy Policy".
That document has not been written yet. Writing it is legal work, not
engineering work.

Before this version is published, one of two things must happen:

- the real Privacy Policy replaces the contents of this file, as a new version
  rather than an edit to a published one; or
- section 19 of the Terms is reworded so it no longer points at a document that
  does not exist.

Until then this file must stay unpinned in `legal/registry.py`, and no consent
record referring to it should be written outside local testing.
